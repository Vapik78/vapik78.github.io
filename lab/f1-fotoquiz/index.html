F1 Photoquiz

Guess the driver from the photo. Play solo against the clock, or run it as a group slideshow with pen and paper.

How it works

  • Choose Normal (untimed) or Kahoot Mode, then pick a theme and how many rounds to play.
  • Each round shows a photo and four name options but only one is correct.
  • Normal Mode: normal mode, what to say,
  • Kahoot Mode: a 15s timer per photo, answer the fastest you can.

How it works

  • Pick a theme, how long each photo stays up, and how many rounds.
  • Grab pen and paper and everyone writes down their guess before time runs out.
  • When the timer ends, the driver's name is revealed so everyone can check themselves.
